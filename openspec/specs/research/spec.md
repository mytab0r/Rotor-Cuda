# Research specification

Research proposals must state hypothesis, source, license compatibility, baseline, fixture, measured hardware/toolchain, and rollback condition. No optimization enters production from a paper or another implementation without local correctness and performance evidence.

Research domains: BSGS table/collision design, GLV and negation maps, affine/Jacobian kernels, batch inversion, warp scheduling, DP filters, Pollard kangaroo variants, persistent kernels, CUDA graphs, driver/toolkit versions, and deterministic crash-safe range allocation.

Security scope remains authorized public puzzle events only. Research must not expand target scope to mass scanning or de-anonymization.
