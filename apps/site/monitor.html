Wallet Cabinet

Cabinet

Watch the addresses you control across EVM, Solana, TRON and Bitcoin, and be warned when something moves without you. This page never asks for a private key, WIF or seed phrase.

An alert is early warning, not protection. Alerts can be delayed or missed, and an idle stolen key is not observable on-chain.

Wallet

No session.

EVM, Solana and TRON sign you in by signing a one-time challenge. WalletConnect connects a mobile EVM wallet over QR; it is EVM-only in this build. Bitcoin cannot sign yet: after you sign in with another chain, its addresses can be imported as unverified. Rotor never asks for a private key, WIF or seed phrase.
