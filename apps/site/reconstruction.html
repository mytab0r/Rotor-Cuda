Cabinet

Byte-fragment assembler

Rebuild a 32-byte key from the fragments you already know. This page assembles bytes at explicit offsets; it does not search for, guess or recover unknown bytes.

32 bytesRuns locallyNo guessing

Scope limit: known-byte assembly only. If bytes are missing, the page lists them and refuses to produce a key. It never claims to have recovered a key from partial information.

Fragments

Offset is the byte position 0-31. Format is hex, bin, dec (one byte, 0-255) or ascii. Overlapping fragments are rejected, and every one of the 32 bytes must be supplied. Lines starting with # are ignored.
